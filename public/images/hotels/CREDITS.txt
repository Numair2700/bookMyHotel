Hotel placeholder photography
==============================

The images in this folder are used only as placeholder hotel photography for the
demo. They are sourced from Pexels.com and used under the Pexels License, which
permits free use for personal and commercial projects with no attribution
required.

Layout
------
  covers/1.jpg … 8.jpg   Exterior / pool shots — represent the hotel itself
                         (home hero, search cards, hotel hero, booking cards).
  rooms/1.jpg  … 4.jpg   Interior shots — used on the room-type cards.

They are mapped to hotels and room types deterministically at runtime (see
resources/js/lib/hotel-image.ts, hotelCover() and roomImage()); no image data is
stored in the database.
