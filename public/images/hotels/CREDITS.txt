Hotel placeholder photography
==============================

The images in this folder (1.jpg … N.jpg) are used only as placeholder hotel
photography for the demo. They are sourced from Pexels.com and used under the
Pexels License, which permits free use for personal and commercial projects with
no attribution required.

Source:  https://www.pexels.com
License: https://www.pexels.com/license/

They are mapped to hotels deterministically at runtime (see
resources/js/lib/hotel-image.ts); no image data is stored in the database.
